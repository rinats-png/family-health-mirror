## Description

```
A diary for whatever you want to write down about your child's health. One tap
and it is in there — with the date and time, exactly as you typed it.

At the next appointment you no longer scroll through your messages. You have
your own notes with you.


WRITTEN DOWN IN TEN SECONDS

Tiles on the home screen for whatever comes up in your household: fever, cough,
runny nose, vomiting, diarrhoea, rash, pain, restless sleep, medicine given,
doctor visit. Rename the tiles, add your own, hide the ones you never use.

One tap creates the record straight away. Note, measured temperature, photo and
tags come afterwards — or not at all.


YOUR RECORDS AT A GLANCE

A calendar in month and year view. Days with records are coloured, using the
colour of the category you picked yourself. Search and filter by period,
category, tag and free text.


MEASUREMENTS AND CURVES

Write down weight, body length and head circumference and see them plotted on a
reference curve. You choose which set of curves is shown; the one in use is
named on the chart.


VACCINATION ENTRIES

Write down what your vaccination booklet says and photograph the pages. That way
your own entries are with you when the booklet is at home.


HAND IT OVER AS A PDF

Pick a period, create a PDF, print it or save it. The document holds your
records as a table — nothing else. It is produced on your device.


YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU

No account. No email address. No server. No adverts. No tracking components.
Everything stays on your device, and you can export or erase all of it at any
time. If you like, a password protects the app; the data is then stored
encrypted on the device.


FREE

One child, unlimited records, calendar, measurements with one set of curves,
vaccination entries, PDF with a watermark.

PRO

Several children, all sets of curves, your own tiles, unlimited photos, PDF
without a watermark, data export as JSON and CSV.


WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO

The application is a private, parent-kept diary for documenting observations,
measured values and events relating to the health of one's own child. It serves
to store and display information entered by the user in an orderly fashion and
to compile it into a printable document that parents may, at their own
discretion, present to a doctor.

The application makes no medical statements, makes no diagnoses, gives no advice
on what to do and does not assess the data entered. It does not replace medical
examination, consultation or treatment.
```
